Proposal: Let’s go pick Sea Urchins!

A low-key coastal adventure, followed by a very deserved seafood dinner.

HSBC SF analyst hangout · by yours truly, Monica
A coastal field trip · Half Moon Bay
01

Why this outing?

Anjali pointed out that we analysts don’t hang out enough outside the office. I’ve been busy and haven’t exactly been great at making new friends outside work, so I’m proposing a fun, local adventure: sea urchins! We’ll get some fresh air, do a little hands-on harvesting where it’s legal and safe, and earn our dinner through teamwork.

Purple sea urchins are native to California, but in some areas their numbers have grown dramatically and they’ve been grazing down kelp forests. They’re delicious, too—and with so many great chefs among us (or at least enthusiastic dinner guests), I think we can turn our catch into a fantastic meal.

The idea: spend about two hours on the coast, then decide together whether to cook, grab food, or make a second stop.

04

What to bring

  • A few sturdy buckets
  • Valid California fishing licenses for anyone harvesting (we’ll confirm requirements)
  • Water-friendly, grippy shoes you don’t mind getting wet
  • Gloves and simple hand tools appropriate for legal collection
  • Warm layers, water, energy, and an appetite!

Safety & stewardship: urchins are not “invasive” as a species; purple urchins are native. We’ll only collect where regulations allow, check marine protected area boundaries and current advisories, and cancel if surf or access is unsafe. No one is expected to harvest.
